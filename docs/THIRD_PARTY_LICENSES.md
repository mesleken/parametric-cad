## Engineering Methodology Disclaimer
All geometric models and preliminary engineering checks generated by PCAD are based on standard closed-form equations (such as Lewis tooth bending, Shigley bolt torque, Roark's formulas, and Bruhn analysis). These calculations are provided for preliminary sizing, layout optimization, and design guidance. Final flight, aerospace, structural, or pressure vessel certification must always be verified by an accredited structural engineer using full Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and physical test coupon validation.
